# Countries That Extend Passport Validity for an Additional Six Months After Expiration

Visitors traveling to the U.S. are required to be in possession of passports that are valid for six months beyond 
the period of their intended stay in the U.S.  Citizens of the countries listed below are exempt from the sixmonth rule and need only have a passport valid for their intended period of stay.

This update **_supersedes_** the list found inside the 2022-2023 Carrier Information Guides, and any previous 
Carrier Liaison Program (CLP) bulletins.
